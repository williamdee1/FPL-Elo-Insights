SquadAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

SquadAI is a Fantasy Premier League companion app. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google account name and email address — collected when you sign in with Google. Used only to identify you within the app and personalise your experience. This information is not stored on any server operated by SquadAI.
  • FPL Team ID — a numeric ID you optionally enter to link your Fantasy Premier League team. Stored locally on your device only (never transmitted to SquadAI servers).

2. Information We Do Not Collect

SquadAI does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any financial information

3. Third-Party Services

The app communicates with the following external services:

  • Fantasy Premier League API (fantasy.premierleague.com) — a public API operated by the Premier League. Used to retrieve player data, fixtures, and your squad. No credentials are sent; the API requires no authentication for the data SquadAI accesses.
  • Google Sign-In — authentication is handled entirely by Google. SquadAI receives only your name and email address from Google upon successful sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • GitHub (raw content) — the app fetches publicly hosted statistical data files from GitHub. No personal information is sent in these requests.

4. Data Storage

All data stored by SquadAI (your Google profile and FPL Team ID) is kept locally on your device using standard platform storage (AsyncStorage). It is not backed up to any SquadAI server. You can clear this data at any time by signing out of the app or clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

SquadAI does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

SquadAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: squad.ai.fpl@gmail.com
