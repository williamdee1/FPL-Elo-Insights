SquadAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SquadAI is a Fantasy Premier League companion app. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Sign-in details — your name and email address from Google, or the identifier (and, the first time, name and email) Apple provides when you sign in with Apple. Used to identify you within the app. Your name and email are kept on your device and are not sent to any server operated by SquadAI.
  • FPL Team ID — a numeric ID you optionally enter to link your Fantasy Premier League team. Stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase records — if you buy an Advisor pass, a record of the purchase (product, date and store transaction) is held by our payment provider, RevenueCat, against an account identifier derived from your sign-in (your Google or Apple account ID, not your email address). This is how a pass follows your login across devices.
  • Crash reports — if the app crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report (error details, app version, device model, operating system and the app's recent screens and network requests) is sent to Sentry so the problem can be fixed. Crash reports do not include your name or email, and current versions of the app remove FPL Team and league IDs from them.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

SquadAI does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Location data
  • Advertising IDs, and no advertising or tracking SDKs are used
  • Payment card or bank details — payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; SquadAI never sees them

3. Third-Party Services

The app communicates with the following external services:

  • Fantasy Premier League API (fantasy.premierleague.com) — a public API operated by the Premier League. Used to retrieve player data, fixtures, and your squad. No credentials are sent.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — authentication is handled by Google or Apple. Their privacy policies apply: policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — process payments for Advisor passes under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat — verifies store purchases and stores the purchase records described above. Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Sentry — receives crash reports. Privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy.
  • GitHub Pages — the app downloads publicly hosted statistics, predictions and the free-access list from GitHub. No personal information is sent in these requests; whether your account has free access is checked on your device.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your sign-in details, FPL Team ID and saved plans are kept locally on your device (AsyncStorage). You can clear them at any time by signing out or clearing the app's storage in your device settings. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your passes and meet tax and accounting obligations. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted.

5. Data Sharing

SquadAI does not sell or rent your personal information, and does not share it with any third party for marketing or advertising. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described.

Your Rights

You can ask us to delete your purchase records or crash reports, or for a copy of the data held about you, by emailing the address below. See also Delete Account & Data.

6. Children's Privacy

SquadAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
