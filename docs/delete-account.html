Delete Account & Data

No server-side data. SquadAI does not store any personal data on external servers. All data is held locally on your device and can be removed instantly at any time.

What data does SquadAI store?

  • Your Google account name and email address (used to identify you while signed in)
  • Your FPL Team ID, if you chose to enter one

Both are stored only on your device. Neither is sent to or held by SquadAI.

How to delete your data

Option 1 — Sign out
Open SquadAI, go to your profile, and sign out. This clears your Google account details and FPL Team ID from the app immediately.

Option 2 — Clear app storage
On Android: Settings → Apps → SquadAI → Storage → Clear storage.
This removes all locally stored data.

Option 3 — Uninstall the app
Uninstalling SquadAI removes all locally stored data from your device.
