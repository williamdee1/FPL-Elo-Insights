Delete Account & Data

Most data stays on your device. Your sign-in details, FPL Team ID and saved plans are held locally and can be removed instantly. If you bought an Advisor pass, a purchase record is held by our payment provider, and crash reports may be held by Sentry; email us to have these deleted.

What data does SquadAI store?

  • Your Google or Apple sign-in name and email address (on your device)
  • Your FPL Team ID and saved plans, if you entered them (on your device)
  • Records of any Advisor passes you bought, held by RevenueCat against your Google or Apple account ID
  • Crash reports, held by Sentry for a limited period (no name or email)

How to delete your data

Option 1 — Sign out
Open SquadAI, go to your profile, and sign out. This clears your Google account details and FPL Team ID from the app immediately.

Option 2 — Clear app storage
On Android: Settings → Apps → SquadAI → Storage → Clear storage.
This removes all locally stored data.

Option 3 — Uninstall the app
Uninstalling SquadAI removes all locally stored data from your device.

Purchase records and crash reports
Email squad.ai.fpl@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and we will delete them within 30 days. Deleting purchase records removes any active pass. Purchase refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Further assistance

If you have any questions or need help, contact us at squad.ai.fpl@gmail.com.
